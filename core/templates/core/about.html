Our Story

Built for the Swat Community

SWAT AUTO was created with a simple mission: to make buying and selling cars in Swat easier, safer, and more transparent.

We noticed that car buyers and sellers in our region had to rely on word-of-mouth, scattered social media posts, and unreliable classifieds. There was no dedicated, trustworthy platform for our community.

That's why we built SWAT AUTO — a modern marketplace designed specifically for the Swat region, connecting buyers with verified sellers and showrooms from Mingora to Kalam.
